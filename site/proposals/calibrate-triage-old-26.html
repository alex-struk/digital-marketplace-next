It is never a way to skip binding work: where the application offers the control under another label, behind a step or as another persona, the answer is adapter-wrong.

When the evidence is genuinely unclear, it is a product-question: a failure wrongly sent to the product owner is answered there, while one wrongly blamed on the adapter comes back from the next binding run unchanged and costs a run to find out.
