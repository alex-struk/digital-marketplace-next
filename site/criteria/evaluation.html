R-5.37 accepted confirmed v1

A panel member who is neither an evaluator nor the chair must be refused by the service when the panel is submitted, with a field-level message identifying the offending member, rather than being allowed through to a database constraint violation.

not testable
The panel a test can submit never holds a member with no role. The three actions that put somebody on a panel — add_panel_member, choose_panel_chair and mark_member_as_chair — each give that member a role, and nothing adds one without, so the offending panel cannot be composed. The outcome is unreadable as well: the panel surfaces name minimum_members_error, duplicate_member_error, non_public_sector_member_error and missing_chair_error, and none of them is a field-level message identifying a member who is neither an evaluator nor the chair. Needs an add_panel_member that can withhold a role, and an observation of that refusal.
